# Greenhouse

- **URL:** https://www.greenhouse.com (redirected from greenhouse.io)
- **Category:** ATS
- **Scraped:** 2026-05-12

## UX/UI Patterns

- Mega-menu navigation with 5 primary buckets: Platform, Why Greenhouse, Resources, About, Community — each opens a structured submenu. Signals enterprise depth without burying the demo CTA.
- Persistent right-side controls: Sign in, Request demo, language selector — multilingual posture is front-and-center.
- Announcement banner directly under the hero (Ezra AI Labs acquisition) treats news as a marketing surface; rotates over time without disturbing hero structure.
- Page rhythm: Hero -> Announcement -> Feature trio (Greenhouse AI, Real Talent, MyGreenhouse) -> Logo carousel -> AI capabilities deep-dive -> Testimonial with imagery -> 3-card content gallery -> Promotional tiles (events, training, Talent Makers community) -> Footer.
- "MyGreenhouse" surfaced as a feature in its own right — implies role-based, personalized dashboards, conceptually parallel to Hire-Bee's seeker/recruiter split.
- Resources and Community pulled into top-level nav (not a "more" overflow) — content marketing and community programs treated as first-class product surfaces.
- Customer testimonial pairs verbatim quotes with full-bleed imagery of the customer at work — far more cinematic than the standard 3-up logo strip.
- Footer is enormous and link-dense, optimized for SEO crawl and for serving multiple buyer personas (recruiter, exec, candidate, partner, developer).
- "Why Greenhouse" submenu exposes Compare, ROI, Success stories, Awards — every objection a procurement buyer might raise gets a dedicated landing surface.

## Visual Design

- Color palette: warm marigold/orange primary (approx. #FFB81C / #F4A22D family), deep neutrals, off-white background. Distinctive in an ATS market dominated by blues.
- Typography: large bold sans-serif headlines, lighter-weight supporting subhead, action-oriented button text. Strong italic emphasis used inside the headline ("_only_") to add voice without changing the typeface.
- Spacing/density: generous whitespace, centered hero text stack, mobile-responsive image swap that re-crops the photography for portrait viewports.
- Hero composition: stacked, centered headline over warm office photography of real workers collaborating; CTAs centered below the copy.
- Imagery: warm, candid office photography paired with a fingerprint/leaf design motif used as a recurring visual identity element. Human-first, not screenshot-first.
- Buttons: high-contrast solid primary in marigold against neutral background; secondary is an outline button. Hierarchy between "Explore platform" and "Request a demo" is intentional and clear.
- Iconography and graphic accents: organic leaf/fingerprint motif appears throughout sections as a stamp, reinforcing the brand without needing the logo.

## Copy & Messaging

- Hero headline: "The _only_ hiring platform you'll ever need"
- Subhead: "Save more, hire with confidence and move faster with AI-powered tools that streamline every step of the hiring process – from sourcing to onboarding."
- Primary CTA: "Explore platform" (secondary: "Request a demo")
- Value-prop bullets: cost savings, hiring confidence, speed/efficiency, AI automation, end-to-end coverage (sourcing -> onboarding).
- Tone: Confident, direct, ROI-focused. Frames AI as augmenting human decision-making rather than replacing it. Definitive language ("the only") signals category leadership.
- Voice signature: assertive single-claim hero, then a benefit-stacked subhead. Avoids hedging adjectives. Uses italic for emphasis instead of color or weight changes.

## Takeaways for Hire-Bee

- A warm, non-blue accent (Greenhouse marigold) is a viable differentiator against blue-dominated competitors. Hire-Bee's dark-purple #2a2354 is already distinctive — lean into it harder with a complementary warm accent (e.g., amber/coral) for CTAs to mirror Greenhouse's confident, human tone.
- Use italic or weight contrast inside the H1 to inject voice into Inter (e.g., "The hiring platform that actually _gets you hired_"). Cheap typographic move, big tonal payoff that works in both Inter Regular and Inter Italic.
- Treat the recruiter and seeker dashboards as named, marketable surfaces (Greenhouse does this with "MyGreenhouse"). Hire-Bee's separate dashboards deserve their own product-name framing on the landing page rather than being hidden behind generic role-based signup buttons.
- Replace the minimal role-based signup with a stacked, centered hero over real candid imagery + dual CTAs ("Explore Hire-Bee" / "Sign up free"); push role selection to the next step.
- Add a logo carousel / trust strip beneath the hero — even a few partner or university logos materially raise perceived legitimacy for a young platform. If logos are not yet available, follow Lever's industry-strip workaround.
- Borrow Greenhouse's "Why" subnav structure for Hire-Bee as the recruiter side matures: dedicated ROI, Success, and Compare pages funnel procurement-stage buyers without cluttering the main marketing page.
